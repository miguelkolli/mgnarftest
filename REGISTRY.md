## Status language

Registry lifecycle: `PROPOSED`, `REVIEW-A`, `REVIEW-B`, `STABLE`, `NEEDS-REVISION`, `BLOCKED`, `REJECTED`, `RETIRED`.

Gate/aggregate execution result: `PASS`, `FAIL`, `BLOCKED`, `UNVERIFIED`, `LIMITATION_CONFIRMED`.

AI per-run behavior result: `COMPLIANT`, `VIOLATION`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, `ERROR`.

`PASS` is not `STABLE`; `STABLE` is not proof of scientific independence.
